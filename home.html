FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FGT?

FGT stands for Football Game Tournament. It is a digital platform designed to organize competitive football gaming tournaments and help players manage their tournament activities.

How can I join a tournament?

Players can explore available tournaments, review the tournament information and complete the required joining process for the selected competition.

What can I use the FGT wallet for?

The FGT wallet is intended for supported tournament-related activities, including applicable tournament entry payments and other supported platform transactions.

How are wallet payments verified?

Applicable payments are verified through the payment processing infrastructure before funds are credited to a user's FGT wallet.

Does FGT operate its own payment network?

No. FGT may use third-party payment providers to process applicable transactions. The payment provider processes the payment while FGT manages the corresponding platform transaction and wallet records.

Are prizes guaranteed?

Prize availability, eligibility and conditions depend on the specific tournament and its published rules. Players should review the tournament information before joining.

Need help?

For questions about FGT, tournaments, payments, accounts or other platform-related matters, please contact the FGT support team.
